# Creative Direction — Lumière Atelier + Kinetic Editorial

## Strategic Positioning
AERA Beauty Studio unites architectural salon entry physics with living, human-centered hair and skin editorial storytelling. Unlike traditional bridal/fashion e-commerce boutiques (specifically differentiated from `shreeji-fashion-puce.vercel.app`), AERA replaces heavy burgundy/sand Didone layouts and `Quick View` retail grids with luminous daylight, soft lilac and peach-champagne atmospheric washes, sculptural sans display typography, and interactive environmental storytelling.

## Non-Negotiable Principles
1. **Alive, Not Empty**: Hero compositions, living mirror reflections, transformations, and lookbook plates foreground human beauty, natural texture, and craft.
2. **Closed-to-Open Arrival**: First visit begins behind closed walnut-and-fluted-glass doors, smoothly swings open automatically in 3D perspective (`~2.75s`), and steps forward into the live studio.
3. **Environmental Integration**: Services live inside a panoramic studio mirror wall where warm human reflections sit behind a delicate frosted veil rather than boxy rounded-top cards.
4. **70 / 20 / 10 Visual Budget**: ~70% breathable calm layouts, ~20% tactile refined motion (scroll parallax, drawers, silk wipes), ~10% standout signature choreography.
5. **Internal Page Parity**: `/services`, `/team`, `/bridal`, `/gallery`, `/about`, and `/enquire` match the homepage's layout originality, typography rhythm, and polish.
